OUR AUTHENTIC CRAFT

How We Make Your Turmeric Powder

A transparent 5-step journey done by our own hands right on the farm.

01
Step 1: Cultivation & Harvest

Grown With Care in Our Own Soil

We plant healthy turmeric rhizomes in our farm fields beneath coconut trees, nourished by clean irrigation channels. When the plants reach full seasonal maturity, we harvest the fresh, dense turmeric fingers directly from the soil.

  • 100% single-farm cultivated with pure irrigation
  • Harvested at peak maturity for maximum natural curcumin
02
Step 2: Traditional Fire Boiling

Boiled in Traditional Farm Kettles

Freshly harvested rhizomes are placed into large boiling drums heated over traditional wood fires. Steam-curing removes the raw sharpness, develops the rich golden orange core, and locks in the natural healing oils.

  • Traditional slow wood-fire boiling in farm backyard
  • Eliminates raw moisture & activates curcumin
03
Step 3: Natural Sun-Drying

Naturally Cured Under Direct Sunlight

No chemical dehydrators or electric hot-air chambers. We spread the boiled turmeric across our open farm ground under the warm sunshine for 10 to 15 days until every root is thoroughly snap-dry.

  • 100% natural solar heat curing
  • Preserves natural aroma without burning essential nutrients
04
Step 4: Hand Cleaning & Sorting

Carefully Cleaned & Hand-Inspected

Before milling, we personally clean and sort every batch. Dried husks, rootlets, and small dirt particles are carefully separated to ensure only clean, flawless golden turmeric enters the grinding stage.

  • Thoroughly cleaned to remove grit and soil
  • Hand-sorted into long fingers (Viral) and round bulbs (Kundu)
05
Step 5: Direct Mill Grinding

Ground by Us in Traditional Mills

We take our cleaned, sun-dried turmeric directly to the grinding machines in local grain and spice mills. Grinding under our own eyes ensures nothing is added or adulterated—yielding pure, fine, intensely fragrant turmeric powder.

  • Freshly milled in dedicated spice machinery
  • 100% pure whole turmeric—zero preservatives or starches

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know this turmeric is 100% pure?

Because we do the entire process ourselves: we farm it, harvest it, boil it in kettles, dry it under sunlight, clean it, and grind it at the mill. We never buy commercial warehouse lots or add dyes, flours, or preservatives.

Why do you boil the turmeric before sun-drying?

Traditional wood-fire boiling cures the rhizomes. It eliminates raw bitterness, distributes the natural curcumin evenly through the root, and gives the powder its deep vibrant golden character.
