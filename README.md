# Anaiyan Traders - Turmeric Powder Showcase Website

A web page designed specifically for **Anaiyan Traders** to showcase your authentic, farmer-crafted turmeric powder.

---

## 🌾 The Authentic Business Journey Highlighted
The website highlights your exact farm-to-table process:
1. **Grown in Your Own Farm**: Cultivated in natural fertile soil beneath coconut palms with clean irrigation.
2. **Traditional Fire Boiling**: Boiled in traditional vessels over wood fires to cure the roots, lock in curcumin, and activate rich golden color.
3. **Natural Sun-Drying**: Cured naturally across your open farm courtyard under direct sunlight.
4. **Meticulous Hand-Cleaning & Sorting**: Fresh & dried roots thoroughly cleaned of soil, dried husks, and sorted by hand into long fingers and round bulbs.
5. **Direct Mill Grinding**: Ground by you at local grinding mills for unbeatable freshness, essential oils, and aroma.
6. **Direct-to-Customer Orders**: Packed in airtight pouches and ordered directly on WhatsApp with zero middlemen.

## 📸 How to Add Your Photos
When you create the folder named `images` inside this project directory, save your photos with these exact names:

| File Name | Your Photo Subject | Where it Displays on Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `images/farm.jpg` | Your turmeric plants growing in the farm field with irrigation | Step 1 & Farm Gallery Card 1 |
| `images/boiling.jpg` | The boiling drums over wood fires on the farm | Step 2 & Farm Gallery Card 2 |
| `images/sundrying.jpg` | Turmeric fingers drying on the farm ground under open sun | Step 3 & Farm Gallery Card 3 |
| `images/cleaning.jpg` | Hand cleaning and sorting roots in the basin | Step 4 & Farm Gallery Card 4 |
| `images/dried_roots.jpg` | Cured dried turmeric roots in the large metal basin | Step 5, Hero Section & Large Gallery Feature |

> **Note:** The website has built-in fallback cards, so it will look clean while you set up your files. The moment you drop the files into the `images` folder, they will automatically appear!
